# ADR-<NNN>: <Decision title>

* Status: proposed
* Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>

Status is one of `proposed`, `accepted`, `superseded`, or `rejected`. A Story
may reference an `accepted` record; only an `architecture` or `mixed` Story may
reference a `proposed` one. Store the file as
`specs/decisions/ADR-<NNN>-<slug>.md`.

## Context

The recurring engineering problem and the alternatives considered.

## Decision

The chosen approach, stated so a later reader can tell whether a change
contradicts it.

* `BoundaryName` does not own the responsibilities listed here.

## Consequences

What this costs, and what it gives up.

## Falsified if

The condition under which this decision would no longer hold, named in terms
something can check.
