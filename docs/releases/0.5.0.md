# ForgeFlow 0.5.0

Execution governance and evidence-backed completion. **Additive**: every new
declaration is optional and defaulted, existing Stories and adoptions keep their
verdicts, and no migration is required. See
[Protocol Versioning](../../protocol/versioning.md) for the classification and
the optional adopter steps.

## What is new

* **Task mode.** A Story may declare `architecture`, `execution`, `evidence`, or
  `mixed` in `## Classification`. `evidence` never authorizes repository
  mutation. The default is `execution`.
* **Authority.** An optional `## Authority` section declares `plan`, `modify`,
  `add_dependency`, `migration`, `commit`, `push`, and `deploy` separately. One
  grant never implies the next, and everything except planning — and
  implementation for an execution Story — defaults to no.
* **Risk.** An optional `## Risk` section declares a level and its reasons. A
  recognized high-risk signal may not be filed below `high`. Risk raises
  verification depth, never implementation scope.
* **Architecture metadata and decisions.** An optional `## Architecture` section
  names impact, decisions, boundaries, contracts, and ownership. Decision records
  live under `specs/decisions/`, and a referenced record must exist, resolve
  uniquely, and be usable.
* **Verification profiles.** The declared risk selects the required checks, and
  medium or high architecture impact adds an `architecture` layer.
* **Recorded verification result.** An optional `verification.md` per Story
  records each check, traces each acceptance criterion to an observation, lists
  the authority actually used, and retains residual risk.
* **`scripts/verification-check`.** Resolves the execution contract and the
  profile, and with `--result` reports `VERIFICATION_PASS`,
  `VERIFICATION_PARTIAL`, or `VERIFICATION_FAIL`.
* **Execution invariants.** Eight invariants that hold for every ForgeFlow
  implementation, in [the Execution Contract](../../protocol/execution.md).

## What did not change

`make verify` is still the only canonical gate, and its PASS, FAIL, and Repair
Loop semantics are unchanged. `scripts/story-check`, `scripts/handoff-check`,
Repository Doctor, and bootstrap keep their command forms, result names, and
exit statuses. ForgeFlow still adds no workflow engine, state database, or agent
runtime, and depends on no external skill collection.

## Known limits

The checkers validate declarations, not their truthfulness. A Story can
understate its risk, misdeclare its task mode, or record an observation that
does not prove its criterion, and still pass. Human Review remains the authority
on all three. The `architecture` layer resolves references only; dependency
direction, forbidden imports, layer boundaries, and contract drift are named as
future extensions and are not implemented.
